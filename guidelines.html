Community Guidelines

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Manifest Sky is a quiet, anonymous space where people share hopes, dreams and intentions — and quietly root for each other.

To keep it that way, you must not post:

  • Threats or incitement of violence
  • Harassment, bullying or targeting of any person
  • Hate speech or discriminatory content
  • Sexual or sexually explicit content
  • Personal information — yours or anyone else's (names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, social handles)
  • Spam, advertising or self-promotion
  • Illegal content or content promoting illegal activity
  • Content encouraging self-harm or harm to others

A note on manifestations

Manifestations are personal hopes and intentions, not guarantees. Manifest Sky makes no claim that writing something down will cause it to happen. This app exists to help you articulate what you want and to let strangers quietly encourage you.

Reporting

Every manifestation has a ••• menu where you can Report or Hide it. Reported content is hidden from you immediately and reviewed. Accounts that repeatedly violate these guidelines may have content removed or access withdrawn.

Anonymity

Everyone here is anonymous. Do not attempt to identify other users, and do not include information that identifies you.
