Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how MYNEGY LTD (company no. 17395048, registered at 8 Heol-y-beddau, Caerffili, CF83 1AH, United Kingdom) ("we") handles information in the Manifest Sky app.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account identifier created automatically when you first open the app. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or photo.
  • The manifestations you write, including their category and status.
  • Energy you send and receive (stored so each person can send Energy once per manifestation; senders are never revealed).
  • Reports you submit.
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications.
  • Basic timestamps (when your account was created and last active).

What we do NOT collect

  • Real names, emails, usernames or profile photos
  • Contacts, location, or advertising identifiers
  • Third-party tracking or behavioural advertising data

How information is used

  • To display anonymous manifestations to other users
  • To deliver notifications when your manifestation receives Energy
  • To operate moderation and safety features
  • To maintain your session across app launches

Anonymity

Your manifestations are shown publicly without any identity attached. Other users can never see who wrote a manifestation or who sent Energy.

Data storage

Data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security. Push notifications are delivered via Expo's notification service and Apple/Google push services.

Account deletion

Settings → Delete Account permanently deletes your account, your manifestations, Energy records, reports, and push tokens. This is irreversible.

Children

Manifest Sky is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction).
