Frequently asked questions

Is Manifest Sky really anonymous?

Yes. There are no accounts to create, no names, no emails, no profiles. Your manifestations are shown without any identity attached, and nobody can see who sent Energy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, manifestations, Energy records, reports and notification tokens. It cannot be undone.

I saw something that shouldn't be there.

Tap the ••• menu on any manifestation to Report or Hide it. Reported content is hidden from you immediately and reviewed by moderation. You can also email us.

Why didn't I get a notification?

Check that notifications are enabled for Manifest Sky in your device settings, and that you haven't turned them off in the app's Settings.

Does writing a manifestation make it happen?

Manifestations are hopes and intentions, not guarantees. The app exists to help you articulate what you want and to let strangers quietly root for you.
